WHAT IT INSPECTS

Heuristic URL red flags

  • Unencrypted HTTP transport & non-standard ports
  • Embedded credentials or deceptive @ authority syntax
  • Punycode (xn--) homoglyph internationalized labels
  • Direct IPv4/IPv6 hosts & deep subdomain nesting
  • URL shorteners, high-entropy paths & executable extensions
  • Known tracking query parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid)
WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

Honest boundaries

  • It never fetches, resolves, or visits the entered URL.
  • It does not query external threat-intelligence APIs.
  • It cannot verify domain ownership or TLS certificates.
  • It cannot detect compromised legitimate websites.
  • Everything runs offline inside your browser tab.
